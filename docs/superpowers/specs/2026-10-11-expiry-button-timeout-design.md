and Keep permanently for six hours from presentation/delivery. Their Discord and

must not renew the six-hour deadline. Confirmation and other menus retain their
five-minute inactivity window. Ownership checks, retention policy and Watch links
remain unchanged. Existing expired controls are not revived.
