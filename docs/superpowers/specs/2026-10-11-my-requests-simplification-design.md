# My requests simplification

Approved for Discord and Telegram: show Extend 7 days, Extend 30 days and Keep
permanently directly on a selected title; hide redundant retention actions when
all owned requests are permanent. Extension buttons require a fresh eligible timed
file. Availability and expiry lead a compact card; Details reveals existing counts,
quality/progress and retention diagnostics. Details/Back edit the same owned post.
Confirmation buttons identify the proposed date (earliest date for multiple files)
or Keep permanently. Unavailable dates use Extend N days; final backend rechecks
remain authoritative. Custom /extend and legacy callbacks stay supported. Five-minute
menu and confirmation windows and six-hour notification buttons remain unchanged.

Live status supplies structured summary and applicable-action metadata to My requests.
The transport layers only render and validate the new callback names. Deployment uses
scoped policy replacement preserving credentials, bindings, graphs and retention writes.
